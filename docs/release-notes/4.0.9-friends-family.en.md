# Release notes 4.0.9 — Friends & Family (final)

**RPP 4.0.9** is the **final Friends & Family** release: operational resource and presence planning in Microsoft Teams — stabilised, explained in plain language, and sharpened for day-to-day team use.

The Info page badge remains **Friends & Family**. The technical version is **4.0.9**.

---

## What RPP does for you

Inside Teams, RPP answers: **Who is available when — and does the team have enough capacity?**

| Area | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Timeline with absences, filters, half-days and optional vacation figures (E/B/R) |
| **Team capacity** | Weekly view with traffic light; **required staffing** and thresholds drive warning/critical |
| **Approvals** | Your requests and status in RPP; **decisions** in the Teams **Approvals** app |
| **Reports** | Cross-team and organisation overview |
| **Settings** | Personal approver (when allowed), notifications, Outlook sync |
| **Help ?** | Assistant grounded in curated docs — how to use RPP, not ops secrets |
| **Info** | Short product info and version |

---

## For everyone

### Planning and absences

- Create and edit absences on **Overview** and see them over time.
- **Half-days** via morning/afternoon halves.
- Vacation balance is **calculated** (do not “fix” a stored balance).
- **Outlook sync** is optional — often off by default; the team may set optional, mandatory, or disabled.

### Approvals

- Whether approval is required depends on team policy, absence type, and optional personal exemption.
- Approvers decide in **Microsoft Teams → Approvals** (not a custom RPP inbox).
- Requests and status stay visible in RPP; dates in Approvals are easier to read.

### Team context and visibility

- Choose the **active team** at the top if you belong to several teams.
- **Primary team** (Default team): timeline row and capacity counting.
- **Secondary teams / additional positions**: extra membership without double-counting capacity.
- Planning data in team context: visible to members of that planning team; leaving the M365 team removes you from that active planning view.

### Help and wording

- Help assistant with an updated knowledge base (EN/DE).
- Info page in clearer, less technical language.
- Release and getting-started texts aligned with Friends & Family.

---

## For team owners

In **Team Admin Center** (only if you are Owner of the active Microsoft Teams team):

- Default and backup approver, approval policy  
- Absence types (approval required, vacation balance)  
- **Required staffing** and display options  
- Team Outlook policy  
- Members: Default team, additional positions, employment rate  
- Manage internal planning teams within your rights  
- Holiday / school-holiday calendars where enabled  

**Help** only shows owner how-to when you are recognised as team owner (or app admin). Employees without Owner get a clear pointer to ask their owner or IT — not admin procedures.

---

## Stability you may notice

- More reliable saving of absences and requests (including date handling on some hosts).
- Clearer error messages instead of a generic “unknown”.
- More robust approver selection and request start.
- Company and location from the Microsoft 365 profile more reliably in planning.
- Planning access tied to **team membership**: team data for team members — not the whole organisation without context.

Technical diagnostics stay on **Info** or with IT; they are not part of everyday help.

---

## Deliberately unchanged

- **No** custom approval inbox, reminders, or delegation in RPP — that stays Microsoft Approvals / your organisation’s process.
- **No** language switcher in RPP — language from Microsoft 365 / Teams.
- **App Admin Center** for IT only.
- If a vacation request exceeds balance, RPP may warn without hard-blocking (depending on configuration and build).

## After updating

1. Reload Teams or refresh the app.  
2. Check the active team.  
3. Open the timeline and — if you are an owner — Team Admin Center.  
4. For help: the question mark in the navigation; after major doc updates IT re-indexes the help assistant.
