# Frequently asked questions

## How do I book a half day?
Same day, start and end both Morning or both Afternoon.

## Why does my request need approval?
Team policy, absence type, and optional personal exemption. Decision in the Teams **Approvals** app.

## What does secondary team / additional positions mean?
Any team other than the primary (Default team). Capacity and timeline row follow the primary team.

## Why is capacity red?
Critical utilization threshold or missed **required staffing** on a day that week.

## Why is a person missing on the timeline?
Filter, period, team assignment, collapsed group, secondary-only assignment, or not an M365 team member.

## Absence vs presence?
Absence = not here (may need approval, reduces capacity). Presence = here but committed (e.g. home office, project).

## Can I change the language?
Not inside RPP — language comes from Microsoft 365 / Teams.

## Who can see my absences?
Members of the same planning team in the RPP context; team owners manage rules. Details depend on your organisation’s policy and team membership.
