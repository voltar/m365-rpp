# Team Admin Center for team owners

This topic is **only for team owners** (Owner of the active Microsoft Teams team). Other roles do not see Team Admin Center and should not receive this admin guidance.

## Who can open it?

- You must be an **Owner** of the active M365 team.
- Guests are never team administrators in RPP.
- Without the role: the nav entry is hidden or you see **Access denied**.

## What it is for

You control **planning rules for your team** — not the whole organisation and not IT deployment.

Typical sections:

### Team selection
Switch between teams you may manage. The **active team** at the top of RPP should match the team you edit.

### Approval policy
- Default and backup approver.
- Whether members may pick another approver.
- Affects new requests and personal settings.

### Outlook synchronization (team policy)
Optional, mandatory, or disabled — overrides the personal preference when set.

### Absence types
Which types are active, whether they need **approval**, and whether they **consume vacation balance**.

### Required staffing
How many people should be present at minimum. Missing it can mark weeks **critical (red)** in Team capacity even when utilization looks fine.

### Team members
- **Default team** (primary) and **additional positions**.
- Employment rate, vacation figures, approval exemption, effective approver.
- Assign people from the M365 team into planning or remove them from RPP planning (they may remain in the M365 team).

### Manage teams
Create, rename, reorder internal planning teams within your rights and host team.

### Display
Options such as vacation figures on the timeline or event colours (by type or team), depending on the installation.

### Holiday and school-holiday calendars
Maintain or refresh calendars where enabled — affects duration and capacity.

## Good practice

- Set approver and backup deliberately and tell the team.
- Keep required staffing realistic (avoid permanent red).
- Keep each person’s primary team clear — otherwise they “disappear” under another team on the timeline.
- Do not add people to an M365 team only for RPP visibility without considering Teams side effects (chat, files); members can leave the team in Microsoft Teams.

## Out of scope here

- App-wide IT configuration → **App Admin Center**.
- Deployment, servers, secrets → IT.
- Deciding individual requests → Teams **Approvals** app.
