# Outlook synchronization

Approved absences can optionally appear in the **Outlook calendar**.

## Personal

In **Settings** under **Outlook calendar**: option for approved vacation/absences with Outlook. The absence form often has **Synchronize with Outlook** as well.

## Team policy

The team may set Outlook sync to **optional**, **mandatory**, or **disabled**. The personal switch may then be locked.

## What to expect

- After approval, a calendar item may be created or updated.
- Rejection or deletion may remove or adjust the item.
- RPP shows status when sync is running or failed.

RPP does not replace the whole Outlook calendar — only RPP-managed absences within configuration.
