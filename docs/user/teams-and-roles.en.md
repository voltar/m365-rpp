# Teams, organisations and roles

## Primary and secondary teams

A person can belong to **several teams**.

- **Primary team** / in Team Admin Center **Default team**: where they appear on the timeline; where capacity counts.
- **Secondary teams** / **Additional positions**: extra collaboration without double-counting capacity.

“Secondary team” means any team that is **not** the primary one.

## Organisations and locations

Company and location often come from the Microsoft 365 profile and can be mapped in RPP. If values look wrong, check the profile or team administration.

## Roles in brief

| Role | Typically |
|---|---|
| Employees | Timeline, own absences, approvals, settings |
| Team owner (Owner in the M365 team) | plus **Team Admin Center** |
| IT / app admin | **App Admin Center** |

Roles come from Microsoft 365 / Teams — not a separate RPP login.

## Active team

Next to the navigation: **active team**. It sets the planning context and which team is edited in Team Admin Center.
