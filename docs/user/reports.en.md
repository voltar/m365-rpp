# Reports and analyses

**Reports** summarise planning across teams and organisations: who belongs where, which weeks are critical, how utilization trends.

## What you can use

- People overview with filters (organisation, location, team).
- Critical or warning weeks.
- The same capacity logic as **Team capacity** — not a second “truth”.

## Who it is for

Anyone who needs the big picture. Per-team rules stay in Team Admin Center; Reports do not replace the operational timeline.
