# Team capacity

**Team capacity** shows week by week how loadable the team is and where bottlenecks appear.

## Traffic light

- **OK** — enough availability.
- **Warning** — below the warning threshold (often around 70% availability by default — configurable).
- **Critical (red)** — below the critical threshold **or** **required staffing** is missed on at least one day of the week.

Red can appear even when utilization looks fine if too few people are present.

## What counts

- Members’ employment rate and working days.
- Absences reduce available capacity.
- Public holidays and configured calendars affect the calculation.
- **Presence events** (e.g. home office, project) mean “here but committed” and usually do **not** reduce capacity like an absence.

## Affected people

**Affected employees** lists who matters in a critical week.

The same engine feeds Reports and the timeline — RPP calculates; it does not store a permanent “capacity number”.
