# Approvals

Under **Approvals** you see **your** requests and their status in RPP.

## Important

- Approvers decide in the Teams **Approvals** app (Received / Sent).
- RPP stores the link and shows status on the timeline and list.
- Approved absences count toward capacity and vacation balance; rejected ones do not count as booked vacation.

## Typical status

Draft → submitted / pending → approved or rejected (or cancelled).

## Who approves?

Default or backup approver from the **Team Admin Center**, or your personal approver in **Settings** if the team allows it.

## Tips

- Wrong approver: check team owner setup or Settings.
- Missing in Approvals: correct account, environment filter in Approvals, and whether the request was really submitted.
