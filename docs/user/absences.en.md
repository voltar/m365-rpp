# Creating and editing absences

Absences mean **not here** (e.g. vacation, compensation, training, military). They may require approval and reduce available capacity.

## Create

1. Open **Overview**.
2. Pick a day on your row or **New absence**.
3. Type, start/end, day halves if needed, comment, substitute.
4. Save or submit for approval — depending on team rules.

## Edit and delete

Click a bar → details panel. Edit/delete depend on status and rights (your own entries or team owner).

## Approval

Whether approval is required depends on:

1. the team approval policy,
2. the setting per **absence type**,
3. a possible personal exemption (**No approval required**).

Decisions are made in the Microsoft Teams **Approvals** app, not in a custom RPP inbox. Track status under **Approvals**.

## Vacation balance

Entitlement, carry-forward and adjustments minus requested/approved vacation. On the timeline often **E / B / R**. Values are **always calculated** — correct the absence, not a “stored balance”.

## Outlook

Approved absences can optionally appear in Outlook (personal setting and team policy). See “Outlook synchronization”.
