# Getting started with RPP

RPP (Resources & Presence Planning) runs inside **Microsoft Teams**. You see who is available when, which absences are planned, and where a team is short-staffed.

You do not sign in separately: RPP uses your Microsoft 365 account.

## Where do I find RPP?

Your organisation makes RPP available in Microsoft Teams. After that the app appears in the Teams navigation. If it is missing, ask IT — installation is not a personal setting.

## Areas of the app

The top navigation depends on your role.

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Planning timeline: people and absences over time. Create and edit absences here. |
| **Team capacity** | Weekly view of capacity and bottlenecks. |
| **Approvals** | Your requests and their status. |
| **Reports** | Analyses across teams and organisations. |
| **Team Admin Center** | Team owners only: rules and members of their team. |
| **App Admin Center** | IT administration only. |
| **Info** | Short product info and version. |
| **Settings** | Personal preferences (approver, notifications, Outlook). |

## Three common tasks

1. **Record an absence** — on **Overview**, click your day or **New absence**.
2. **Track a request** — under **Approvals**. Decisions are made in the Teams **Approvals** app.
3. **Check bottlenecks** — under **Team capacity** (red/yellow/green).

## Tips

- Select the **active team** at the top if you belong to several teams.
- Adjust period and filters if someone seems “missing”.
- Language and time zone come from Microsoft 365 — there is no language switcher in RPP.
