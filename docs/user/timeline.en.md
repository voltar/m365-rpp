# Planning timeline (Overview)

**Overview** is the main plan: people as rows, time as columns, absences as bars.

## What you see

- People in your **active team** (membership from Microsoft Teams).
- Absences and presence events in the selected period.
- Optional vacation figures **E / B / R** (entitlement / booked / remaining) — always **calculated**, never stored separately.

## Common actions

- **New absence:** click a day on your row or use the button.
- **Edit:** click a bar — details open on the right.
- **Period:** e.g. 30 days, month, year.
- **Filter:** search team or person.
- **Grouping:** by teams or people.

## “Why don’t I see a person?”

Check in order:

1. Is a **Filter** search still active?
2. Is the **period** too short?
3. Is the person assigned and a member of the Microsoft Teams team?
4. Are they inside a **collapsed** team group?
5. Is your team only a **secondary team** / **additional position**? They then appear under their **primary team** (Default team), not under every team.

## Half days

Same day, **Morning** twice or **Afternoon** twice → 0.5 days. Multi-day starting afternoon or ending morning: set the matching day half.
