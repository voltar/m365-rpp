# RPP glossary

**Absence** — Not available (vacation, compensation, training, …). May require approval.

**Presence / presence event** — Present but committed (home office, project, on-call, …). Usually no approval and not the same capacity impact as an absence.

**Primary team / Default team** — Main team for timeline row and capacity counting.

**Secondary teams / Additional positions** — Extra teams without double-counting capacity.

**Required staffing** — How many people should be present at least; missing it can mark capacity critical.

**E / B / R** — Vacation figures entitlement / booked / remaining (calculated).

**Active team** — Team context at the top of RPP for planning and admin.

**Team Admin Center** — Surface for team owners.

**Approvals** — Microsoft Teams app for approval decisions.
